THE SOMMELIER METHOD

The 4 Steps of Professional Tasting

01

Warm the Glass

Cup the blue glass in your palm and cover the top with your other hand for 30 seconds to release aromatic volatile esters.

02

Deep Inhale

Bring the glass to your nose and take a deep breath. Look for fresh green aromas: tomato vines, cut grass, artichoke, or citrus.

03

The Strippaggio

Take a small sip and vigorously draw air across your teeth (slurping sound) to aerate the oil across all taste receptors.

04

The Throat Catch

Swallow and note the pleasant tickle or cough in the throat — the definitive hallmark of high oleocanthal polyphenols.
