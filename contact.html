FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Do I need a reservation to taste olive oils? ▼

Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed at our front tasting counter for casual sample tastings. For our structured 75-minute Grand Single-Estate Flight with cheese pairings or Sommelier Masterclasses, reservations are highly encouraged due to intimate 14-guest seating limits.

What is the shelf life of your extra virgin olive oils? ▼

Unopened, our opaque UV-protecting white glass bottles maintain peak quality for 24 months from harvest date. Once opened, we recommend enjoying within 6 months to savor the maximum polyphenol potency and aroma vibrancy.

Can you ship gifts directly to multiple client addresses? ▼

Yes! Our corporate gifting concierge specializes in multi-recipient shipping across all 50 states. Simply share your recipient spreadsheet, and our team will handle individualized calligraphy notes, wax seals, and tracked delivery.

What makes single-estate olive oil different from supermarket brands? ▼

Supermarket oils are typically blends of aged leftover oils from multiple countries, often oxidized during lengthy overseas transit. Solaria & Terra oils come from certified single family estates, harvested early in the season, and milled within 90 minutes with verifiable polyphenol density (>500 mg/kg).
