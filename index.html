THE SOLARIA PHILOSOPHY

Rooted in Mediterranean Soil, Born Under California Sun

Most supermarket olive oils sit in heated cargo ships for 18 months, stripped of their polyphenols and life-giving flavor. We do things differently.

We partner exclusively with multi-generational family groves in Santa Ynez, Tuscany, and Peloponnese. We harvest early in the season when olives are green and nutrient-dense, milling them at temperatures below 78°F without chemical filtration.

Single-Estate Provenance

Exact harvest lot, grove coordinates, and milling date printed on every bottle.

UV Shield Protection
