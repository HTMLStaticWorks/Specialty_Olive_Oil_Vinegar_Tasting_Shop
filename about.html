Single-Estate Provenance

We never blend across unnamed co-ops. Every harvest lot traces directly to a specific grove block in Santa Ynez or our ancestral family parcel outside Lucca.

90-Minute Cold Extraction

Olives begin oxidizing the second they leave the branch. Our on-grove mechanical mill extracts fruit within 90 minutes under nitrogen blankets at 21.5°C.

< 0.14% Ultra-Low Acidity
🪵

Living Barrel Fermentation

Our vinegars age slowly through five-wood battery soleras: oak for vanilla tones, chestnut for rich color, ash for tannins, and cherrywood for lingering sweetness.

Unpasteurized Living Mother
REGENERATIVE STEWARDSHIP

Nurturing the Groves for Future Generations

We believe great olive oil is made in the living soil long before the fruit arrives at the mill.

Our Santa Ynez groves are cultivated without synthetic herbicides or chemical fertilizers. We plant seasonal wildflower and clover cover crops, integrate heritage sheep grazing to naturally enrich soil microbiology, and capture 100% of our pressing water for closed-loop irrigation.

Every bottle is bottled in heavy, UV-shielding custom glass produced with 75% recycled cullet to ensure the precious polyphenols remain untarnished by light.
