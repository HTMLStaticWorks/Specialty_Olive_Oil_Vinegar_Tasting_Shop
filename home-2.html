THE 90-MINUTE COLD EXTRACTION

The Journey from Branch to Bottle

Why does Solaria & Terra taste so vibrant? Time is the greatest enemy of olive oil. Here is our rigorous dawn timeline.

01 6:00 AM • Dawn

Hand Harvest

Gentle branch shaking into organic mesh nets at first sunlight while morning coastal fog keeps olives crisp and cool.

02 7:15 AM • Washing

De-leaf & Purify

Leaves and stems are separated, and fresh whole olives undergo a mountain spring cold-water bath to remove all orchard dust.

03 7:30 AM • Milling

Inert Nitrogen Press

Continuous cold-milling strictly below 75°F under an oxygen-free nitrogen blanket to preserve volatile aromatic esters and polyphenols.

04 8:30 AM • UV Bottling

Opaque Matte Glass

Filled into custom light-shielding matte white glass bottles, hermetically sealed, and lot-numbered with certified harvest date.
