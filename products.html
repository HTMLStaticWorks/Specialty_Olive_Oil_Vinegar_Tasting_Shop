KNOW YOUR HARVEST

The Anatomy of Quality Extra Virgin Oil

🧬

Polyphenol Count

Antioxidant micro-nutrients that create the signature peppery catch in your throat. Our oils range from 480 to 710 mg/kg.

🧪

Oleic Free Acidity

International standard requires < 0.80%. Solaria & Terra oils consistently measure < 0.14% for unprecedented freshness.

🍳

410°F Smoke Point

Because our oils are unrefined with ultra-low free fatty acids, they boast a remarkably high smoke point suitable for roasting and searing.

🛡️

UV Matte Glass

Clear bottles allow ambient light to oxidize delicate polyphenols in weeks. Our custom opaque vessels preserve harvest flavor.
